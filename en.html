Last updated: 18 July 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Controller

The developer of Ravemap. Contact: bomberomapp@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Ravemap shows psytrance and rave parties on a map. Base data comes from Goabase. Optional *INTENSIFY* may add sources such as phangan.events and Psymedia.

3. Data we process

  • Location: used on-device only for nearby/map features. Not stored on our servers.
  • Favorites / settings: stored locally on your phone.
  • Email / feedback: only if you send it yourself via your email app.
  • Map tiles and party data: your device fetches content over the network from our API and map providers.

No user account. No analytics/tracking SDK in the app at this time.

4. Purpose

App functionality (map, filters, favorites) and follow-up on feedback you send.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Third parties may receive technical requests directly from your device when you use online maps or fetch party data.

6. Retention

  • Local: until you clear app data / uninstall.
  • Email: deleted when no longer needed (typically within 12 months).

7. Rights

Access, correction and deletion via email. Complaint: your local data protection authority (Norway: datatilsynet.no).

8. Disclaimer

Nightlife/party content — not intended for children. Times and places come from third-party sources and may be wrong; always check the organizer/source. Ravemap is not an official organizer or spokesperson for Goabase or other sources.
